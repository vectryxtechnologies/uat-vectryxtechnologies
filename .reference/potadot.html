What does Potadot do?

Potadot is a software house in Butterworth, Penang that builds custom business systems for Malaysian companies: ERP systems, online platforms, mobile apps for iOS and Android, and corporate websites. Every system is built from scratch around the client's own workflow.

Do you work with businesses outside Penang?

Yes. Most of our clients are outside Penang, in Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Johor Bahru, Melaka and other states. Our systems are cloud-hosted and we follow the same process for every client, wherever they are in Malaysia.

What is a custom ERP system, and does my business need one?

A custom ERP (enterprise resource planning) system puts your inventory, production, sales, purchasing and HR records into one system built around your own SOP. If your team still tracks daily work in Excel, paper forms or chat messages, and each department keeps separate records, a custom ERP usually saves time and reduces errors.

What is the difference between a ready-made system and a custom system?

A ready-made system makes your business follow the software's workflow. A custom system is designed around your workflow, so it has only the features you need and can grow with you. Potadot builds custom systems only and does not resell ready-made software.

How much does a custom system cost?

The cost depends on how many modules and workflows the system covers. We quote after a requirement gathering session, so you pay for what your business needs instead of a package of features you won't use.
